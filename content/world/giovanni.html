Personality

Outside of her control, Lalowe has gained the reputation of a "wet dog" to people not in the circles of The Adventuring Party, due to her "signature" timid, quiet nature. Due to health needs, Lalowe has always had to take it easy, with anxiety making her feel all the worse for it. Despite that, however, she is quick to demonstrate enough strength to stand up for her friends, her impatience towards others suffering or stagnating is sometimes able to overcome her chronic conditions.

Take away her up hill battles, and give her the people she loves, Lalowe is joyful, even teasingly playful at times, bathing in the love and attention given to her. She may not have the body for it, but it's clear that the people around her have instilled within her the sense to stand up, look at someone directly in the eyes and tell them to fuck off, to even the shock of her loved ones.
