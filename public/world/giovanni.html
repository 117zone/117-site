Giovanni

Gio is a carriage driver with not much else going on in his life, compared to his half siblings and most of the people riding on his work hours. At least, as far as his day to day life. One of his parents might be orchestrating some terrible evils, but he's never met his dad, so it's fine, right?

To be honest, Gio hasn't had a lot of time in the oven to cook as more than just a side character! He's a bit flat at the moment but with time he's gonna become more of a person.

Personality

Socially awkward and doesn't realize it. He doesn't know that the way he comes off to other people oozes with sleaze. He does mean well, but he doesn't quite 'get it.' He's been written up at work for this more times than he can count and it still isn't clicking.

If you can get over that, he's not terrible to be around!
