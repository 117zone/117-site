Sandy Chambers

Making a complete 180 in their life, Sandy went from theatrical criminal to becoming one of the heroes that saved the world from the calamity. Many long months after having returned to the criminal scene since the calamity, they're unexpectedly saved by old friends, having got themselves stuck in a risky situation. Now reunited with Val, they've vowed to stick this time, not that Val would be keen on letting them go again.

Personality

From their spells to their schemes, everything Sandy does all show one thing; They're a showman at their core. Ever since their childhood, Sandy has had a knack for the elaborate, stylish and fun, facilitating a devil-may-care attitude that's been with them ever since. With this, paired with a heart of gold, Sandy had a reputation of being a menace to not just the authority but to the criminal underworld as well. People of both parties say they're a waste of talent, but the calamity sure proved them wrong.

The calamity would bring out Sandy's heart of gold and love for theatrics into the forefront, but with it bring out the worst in their friend Leon, and realising that their new found friends would unfairly face the consequences of Sandy's actions during their criminal career, it introduced to the party, and especially Val, a more sensitive and concerned side of Sandy. A side that Val saw when Sandy left the party after everything was said and done, to *properly* confront the criminal landscape.

Now that Lalowe and in turn Val have finally slapped some sense into them, almost a year later, they're back to their usual self; The risk-taking, fun loving rogue.

Relations

Val got to have Sandy be his first love since he left his ex to go and adventure. Initially, it was in the language of them having a "deal" to help Val have a consistent amount of energy throughout their quest. But almost immediately, what started as just helping a fellow out became a deep, genuine love for each other. The closer the party got to Balmordeal however, their relationship slowly started to strain once Sandy realised they'd encounter major figures in the underground world. Thus, once the evil was defeated, they made the decision to leave everything behind to face the consequences of their actions with their new found bravery.

Almost a year would pass until, after Val recalled his relationship with Sandy to Lalowe, she pulled Sandy back into Val's life through investigative work and bloodshed. Now clingier than before, Sandy has no intention of leaving Val this time.

Lalowe, alongside her aunties, and Val, were the very people who saved Sandy from their predicament. If they weren't already happy to see Lalowe during the calamity, they're definitely happy now. They have a lot of affection for her, acting more like their cool uncle who sneaks them stuff while the others aren't looking. (As if they don't do that for the others already.)
